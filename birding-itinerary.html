Discover Africa's Premier Birding Destination

Uganda stands as one of the world's most extraordinary birding destinations, with over 1,060 recorded species representing more than half of Africa's total avian diversity. From the elusive Shoebill stork gliding through papyrus swamps to the vibrant Albertine Rift endemics hidden in misty montane forests, Uganda offers an unparalleled birding safari experience for twitchers, photographers, and nature enthusiasts alike.

Our expert-guided birding itineraries take you through diverse ecosystems—from the wetlands of Lake Victoria and the savannahs of Queen Elizabeth National Park to the dense rainforests of Bwindi and the remote peaks of the Rwenzori Mountains. Each habitat harbors unique species, ensuring that every dawn chorus and dusk flight reveals new wonders in this ornithological paradise.

1,060+ Species

Rare Shoebill

Migratory Birds

Important stopover for Palearctic and intra-African migratory species throughout the year.

Prime Habitats

Top Birding Destinations

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and avian paradise with 350+ species including 23 Albertine Rift endemics. Famous for the African Green Broadbill, Shelley's Crimsonwing, and countless forest specialists.

23 Albertine Endemics | 350+ Species

Queen Elizabeth National Park

Diverse habitats from savannah to wetlands support 600+ species. The Kazinga Channel is legendary for waterbirds including African Fish Eagle, Goliath Heron, and Pied Kingfisher.

600+ Species | Waterbird Paradise

Mabamba Swamp

Uganda's premier Shoebill tracking destination on Lake Victoria's shores. This Ramsar wetland hosts the highest concentration of Shoebills plus Papyrus Gonolek and Blue-headed Coucal.

Shoebill Stork | Ramsar Site

Semuliki National Park

The easternmost extension of the Congo Basin rainforest harbors 435+ species including Congo-endemics like the Blue-headed Sunbird and Nkulengu's Rail.

435+ Species | Congo Endemics

Lake Mburo National Park

Uganda's smallest savannah park packs 350+ species including rare birds like the Grey-crowned Crane, African Finfoot, and numerous papyrus specialists.

350+ Species | Rare Cranes

Rwenzori Mountains

The legendary Mountains of the Moon offer high-altitude birding with species like Rwenzori Turaco, Scarlet-tufted Malachite Sunbird, and Alpine Chat above 3,000m.

High-Altitude Specialists
Sample Itinerary

14-Day Comprehensive Birding Safari

Ultimate Uganda Birding Expedition

This comprehensive 14-day birding safari covers Uganda's most productive birding sites, from the papyrus swamps of Lake Victoria to the montane forests of Bwindi and the savannah grasslands of Queen Elizabeth. Designed for serious birders aiming to maximize species count while experiencing Uganda's diverse ecosystems.

Days 1-2: Entebbe & Mabamba Swamp

Shoebill tracking at Mabamba Bay, botanical gardens birding, and acclimatization. Target species: Shoebill, Papyrus Gonolek, Blue-headed Coucal.

Days 3-5: Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Three full days exploring different sectors for Albertine Rift endemics. Target species: African Green Broadbill, Shelley's Crimsonwing, Rwenzori Batis.

Days 6-8: Queen Elizabeth National Park

Savannah and wetland birding with boat cruise on Kazinga Channel. Target species: African Fish Eagle, Goliath Heron, Martial Eagle, 600+ species possible.

Days 9-11: Semuliki National Park

Congo Basin rainforest birding with rare species. Target species: Blue-headed Sunbird, Nkulengu's Rail, Congo-endemics and forest specialists.

Days 12-14: Lake Mburo & Return

Savannah and wetland species including Grey-crowned Crane. Final birding before departure with comprehensive species list compilation.
